Title: Order fulfillment process



Purpose



This BPMN 2.0 diagram models the end-to-end order fulfillment process for a chocolate manufacturing company, from receiving a customer order to delivering the finished product.



Business Scenario



A customer submits an order to the Sales Department.



The Sales Department reviews the order and sends a production request to the Production Department.



The Production Department checks whether the required raw materials are available in the warehouse.



If the materials are unavailable, a purchase request is sent to the Procurement Department, which orders raw materials from the supplier.



After the materials are received and stored in the warehouse, the Production Department performs a quality inspection.



If the raw materials meet quality standards, the product is manufactured and stored in the warehouse.



If the inspection fails, the defective raw materials are returned to the supplier.



Finally, the Logistics Department arranges delivery of the finished products to the customer.
